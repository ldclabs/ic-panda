# Get help with dMsg

Last updated: 2026-10-11.

dMsg is built in public by ICPanda DAO. For a problem you can reproduce, open a GitHub issue. For questions, ask the community.

## Where to ask

- **Bug reports:** [GitHub issues](https://github.com/ldclabs/ic-panda/issues) for problems with the website, the legacy app, the extension or the canisters.
- **Questions and community:** the [ICPanda community on OpenChat](https://oc.app/community/dqcvf-haaaa-aaaar-a5uqq-cai).
- **News and announcements:** [@ICPandaDAO on X](https://x.com/ICPandaDAO).
- **Reaching the DAO:** [al.ink/ICPanda](https://al.ink/ICPanda).

## Legacy app

The legacy app is read-only. Sign in with your existing identity to read old conversations and download the attachments you can access. Keep the original browser and its data, especially if you used Local key mode: keys stored only in a browser you cleared cannot be recovered. Migration to the new dMsg is not available yet.

## Payments and orders

For a handle registration, plan purchase, renewal, refund or paid request, include your account ID, the order or escrow ID, the token ledger, the transaction block index and the approximate time. Do not send another payment to fix a failed order. Refundable amounts return to the account that paid.

## What to include

- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead;
- the exact error message;
- your browser and its version, and the extension version if you use it;
- screenshots, with any secret information removed.

## Never share secrets

Never send anyone your recovery materials, backups, seed phrases, private keys, unlock passwords or login sessions, and never approve a request you did not start. dMsg support will never ask for them. Anyone who asks is not dMsg support.

## Security reports

Do not post vulnerability details in a public issue. Contact us privately through [al.ink/ICPanda](https://al.ink/ICPanda) with a short summary, and we will arrange a secure way to share the details.
