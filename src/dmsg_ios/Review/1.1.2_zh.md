## 审核截图采集

用 TestFlight 在没有有效 Apple 订阅的测试账号中打开“我的 → 套餐与用量”，滚动到各商品。每个商品准备一张原始截图，清晰显示套餐名称、对应权益、年度周期及 Apple 本地化价格按钮。若同一张原始截图完整显示三个商品，可以复用；不必为了截图确认付款。

建议文件名：`subscription-plus.png`、`subscription-pro.png`、`subscription-max.png`。保留设备原始分辨率，避免拉伸、拼接或把测试成功状态伪装成实际购买结果。审核截图与 1024×1024 推广图标是不同字段；上传位置为每个订阅页面底部的“审核信息 → 截屏”。

当前模拟器回归截图验证重试、加载、恢复入口及套餐展示，不作为订阅商品审核截图。正式版“购买暂不可用”的截图也不能展示出售的服务。

Apple 要求：[App Review Screenshot](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/in-app-purchases-and-subscriptions/in-app-purchase-information/) 清晰展示所售商品或服务，并符合 App 支持的截图尺寸；仅供审核，不在商店展示。

## App Review Notes（英文草稿）

将以下内容合入 1.1.2 的 App 审核备注；保留后台已有有效的联系人和其他功能复现资料，不把私人联系方式或凭据复制进仓库。

```text
This submission includes iOS version 1.1.2, the dMsg Membership subscription group, and its first three annual auto-renewable subscriptions:
- Plus: net.dmsg.ios.plus.annual, 1 GiB encrypted cloud storage.
- Pro: net.dmsg.ios.pro.annual, 10 GiB encrypted cloud storage.
- Max: net.dmsg.ios.max.annual, 100 GiB encrypted cloud storage.

Access and purchase steps:
1. Launch dMsg and sign in using a passkey or Internet Identity. For a new account, choose Get started to create the encrypted workspace. A new account's first device does not require approval from another device. To bind a new login identity to an existing account, use Join an existing account; administrator approval is required. Existing accounts use the normal device-authorization flow.
2. Open Account > Plan and usage. On an account without an active Apple subscription, the annual plans show their description, subscription period and localized StoreKit price.
3. Select a plan's price to open Apple's purchase confirmation sheet. After the verified purchase is applied, the current plan and limits update.
4. Restore purchases and Manage subscriptions are on the same screen. For an existing subscription, use Manage subscriptions to change the plan or cancel renewal.

TestFlight and App Review use Apple's sandbox purchases while the app connects to dMsg's production account services. Sandbox entitlements are explicitly marked as test subscriptions and are separate from production purchases.

Each purchase is bound to the dMsg account used at checkout. Restoring does not transfer a subscription to another dMsg account. Family Sharing and multiple-seat purchases are not supported. Deleting a dMsg account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Privacy policy and Terms of service are linked from the Plan and usage screen.

No storage-pack purchase is included in this submission. Paid on-chain letter sending is unavailable on iOS. The independent team-wallet feature is not used to unlock digital services.
```
